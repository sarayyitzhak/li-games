How it works

Three steps a day, no spreadsheets.

  1. Play on LinkedIn

    Solve the day's puzzles in the LinkedIn app or website, as you already do.

  2. Share your result

    Share the result to LI Games, or paste it. The app reads your time on your phone and saves the result, not the text you shared.

  3. Compete in your group

    Daily results, weekly standings, a weekly champion and the history of every finished week build up by themselves.

What you get

  • Private groups

    Invite friends or colleagues with a code. Each group chooses its own games and scoring.

  • Fair weekly scoring

    Points for every puzzle, a weekly champion, and a record of each past week.

  • Statistics and rivalries

    Head-to-head comparisons, records and the rivalries that develop inside a group.

  • Reminders you control

    Optional notifications for results and reminders, which you can turn off at any time.

Sign-in and your data

Sign in with Google so there is no new password to remember. Existing e-mail accounts keep working.

  • From Google we receive your name, e-mail address and profile picture - nothing else. We do not ask for access to your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.
  • We do not sell your data, show advertising or run analytics in the app.
  • Other members of your groups see your display name, avatar and the results and standings in the groups you share.
